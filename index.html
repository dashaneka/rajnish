What is consciousness?

+

It's the raw experience of being. The redness of red, the feeling of warmth, the inner movie that's playing for an audience of one: you. It's the light nobody else can see.

How does it work?

+

Some say it's an emergent property of 86 billion neurons firing in our brain. Others believe it's a fundamental property of the universe that our brain tunes into. The mechanism is the greatest mystery of all.

Why does it make us 'higher' than other animals?

+

It doesn't make us 'higher,' just different. Our consciousness allows for complex abstract thought, self-reflection, and building civilizations. A dog's consciousness is likely a rich world of smells we can't even imagine. It's just a different channel.

...but in the end, we truly know nothing.
